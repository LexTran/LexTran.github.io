Lixing Tan / 谭立行

I am currently working on my Ph.D. (CS) at Beihang University. My research interests focus on cross-modal representation alignment between motion and language, generative artificial intelligence, and medical image processing. I am jointly advised by Associate Researcher Qing Xia & Prof. Aimin Hao in the State Key Laboratory of Virtual Reality Technology and Systems.

I completed my master’s degree under co-supervision of Dr. Shuang Song and Prof. Ruoxiu Xiao at MedAI lab in University of Science and Technology Beijing.

I received my Bachelor of Engineering in College of Big Data and Information Engineering from Guizhou University. I was fortunate to work with Dr. Hanyu Lu and Dr. Rongfen Zhang during my undergraduate study.
