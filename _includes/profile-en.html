Lixing Tan / 谭立行

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Beihang University’s State Key Laboratory of Virtual Reality Technology and Systems, jointly advised by Associate Researcher Qing Xia and Prof. Aimin Hao. I hold a master’s degree from the University of Science and Technology Beijing and a bachelor’s degree from Guizhou University.

I am currently a Research Intern with Canva’s AI Efficiency team, where I contribute to Canva Craft, an image generation model that debuted at No. 4 on DesignArena’s image leaderboard (the top three were all GPT-Image series models). Previously, I interned with the human body team at Meitu’s MT Lab, working on diffusion models and personalized image generation.

My research interests include cross-modal representation alignment between motion and language, generative artificial intelligence, and medical image processing. Recently, I have been collaborating closely with Dr. Yang-Hao Zhou on video generation and understanding.

I have served as a reviewer for Future Generation Computer Systems (FGCS), as recorded on my ORCID profile.

I welcome research collaborations—feel free to get in touch!
